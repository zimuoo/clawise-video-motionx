# Clawise Launch

Purpose: demonstrate an editable MotionX film using original Clawise artwork. This is a motion sample, not a claim that the drawn cards are product screenshots.

Frame:1920×1080,30fps,12s. Dark teal ground, mint accent, large Chinese titles and an Inter wordmark. One message: turn a stated goal into progressive creation and refinement. All assets/fonts are local. No external network, protected character, empirical product metrics or mandatory platform signature.

0–3.5s: title and small abstract C mark; restrained rising motion.
3.5–8s: three workflow cards, staggered entrance; readable hold.
8–12s: Clawise wordmark and closing idea; line reaches its endpoint, then hold.

For another product, replace the wordmark/artwork, all text and source material; inspect long text and final frames before export.
